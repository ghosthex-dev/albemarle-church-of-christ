Core Beliefs & Doctrine

New Testament Christianity — Pure and Simple

We are dedicated to following the pattern of the New Testament church without human traditions, denominational creeds, or sectarian titles.

John 4:24
“God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth.”

True worship requires both genuine spiritual devotion from the heart and strict conformity to God's revealed truth in scripture.

Colossians 3:17
“And whatsoever ye do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and the Father by him.”

We seek divine authority for everything we practice and teach, doing all by the direct mandate and permission of Christ.

1

God’s Eternal Love & Clear Terms

God has been eternally loving towards all of mankind. He sent His very best into a world that did not want to be saved. God has, also, always been very clear as to the terms and conditions regarding man's approach to Him.

Beginning with our original ancestors, Adam and Eve, God has been absolutely specific in letting mankind know what is pleasing and acceptable to Him. John 4:24 tells us that God is seeking true worshipers and these worshipers MUST worship Him in Spirit and Truth. It is so important to know what God requires of all of mankind.

Biblical Foundation • Divine Sovereignty
2

Satisfied With Being "Christians Only"

Those who are truly seeking to please God and make heaven their eternal home are completely satisfied with being "Christians only." The name refers to a desire to be a follower of Christ or to be "Christ-like." This can only be achieved when one desires to rid himself of man-made doctrines and titles and to be humbly called "Christ-one" or Christian.

The name "church of Christ" is not a proper sectarian name as some misunderstand it to be. The name simply means the body of Christ. In New Testament times, the church was called by names such as: the church of the firstborn, the church of God, the church of the Living God, The Way, etc. All of these names are descriptive of Jesus Christ and the work done for all of mankind.

Undenominational • Followers of Christ
3

Simple, God-Centered Worship

If you are seeking to become "Christians Only" and be added to the body of Christ based on obedience of the apostle's doctrine, then look no further. The church of Christ consists of dedicated members who seek to further the borders of Christ's kingdom and to obey humbly all of the mandates that God requires.

The worship of the church of Christ is simple and God-centered. We seek to do all things by the authority of Christ (Col. 3:17). Please come and join us or sit down to study with us in order to discover "What thus says the Lord" in an attempt to develop an intimate relationship with the Creator of the Universe!

Apostolic Pattern • Sincere Fellowship

Over 75 Years of Faithfulness

Our Congregational History

The rich history of the Lord's church in Albemarle — from an old hotel dining room in 1949 to our enduring work today.

Sanctuary • Dedicated August 6, 1961

A Heritage Rooted in Scripture

For over seven decades, Christians in Albemarle have assembled together to teach the simple gospel of Christ. Through years of perseverance, building construction, and spiritual growth, God has continuously blessed this work.

Historical Building Milestone: The present church building on Highway 52 South at Ross Drive was constructed during brother Frank R. Milton's ministry at a cost of $35,000, and the saints met in it for the first time on August 6, 1961.

June 1949

Early Beginnings at the Albemarle Hotel

The church of Christ in Albemarle had its beginning in June 1949, when some five or six members began meeting in the dining room of the old Albemarle Hotel. Two of the first members were J.D. Burton and Odell Hood. For four years the church continued to meet in the hotel and in homes of the members.

53
1953
1953

Kannapolis Fellowship

During 1953 the Christians in Albemarle drove to Kannapolis to worship with the church there, keeping their spiritual resolve strong while praying for the development of a local congregation.

54
1954
1954

Renewal in Downtown Albemarle (Riff's Store)

In 1954 D.L. Taylor and several other members moved to Albemarle and renewed efforts to build the local congregation. For a time the group met in a rented room over Riff's store in downtown Albemarle.

56
Nov 1956
September - November 1956

First Full-Time Preacher & Salisbury Highway

In September 1956 the church hired Roger Hindrix as their first full-time minister. On November 21, 1956 they rented a small building across from the P&D Food Market on the Salisbury Highway. Brother Hindrix worked with the church for about a year. Members from Kannapolis assisted the Albemarle church by driving over on Sunday evenings to lead in the services.

58
1958 - 1960
1958 - 1960

Frank R. Milton Ministry & Steady Growth

There were nine members meeting in June 1958 when Frank R. Milton became the local minister. By 1960 there were 23 members steadily laboring in the kingdom.

61
Aug 1961
August 6, 1961

Construction of Highway 52 South Building

During brother Milton's ministry the present building on Highway 52 South at Ross Drive was constructed at a cost of $35,000. The church met in it for the first time on August 6, 1961. Brother Milton's work with the church continued through August 1963.

Since that time there have been many wonderful members who have attended and worked with the church at Albemarle. We would love to have you come and join us!
